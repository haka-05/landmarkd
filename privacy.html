Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

The short version

Landmarkd helps you find and share NYC photo spots. We collect the account details you give us, the content you create, and your location (only while you're using the app). We don't sell your data and we don't run ads. Questions: support@landmarkd.co.

What we collect

Account: your email address, username, and profile photo, plus a display name and bio if you add them.

Phone number (optional): if you add one at sign-up or in Settings, it's used only so people who have your number in their contacts can find you. It's never shown to anyone.

Content you create: spots you add (including their map coordinates), reviews and their photos, comments, likes, saved folders, and items you add to your Closet.

Location: your device location while the app is open, used to centre the map and to place a pin accurately. We do not track your location in the background.

Photos: images you choose from your library or capture with the camera, for reviews, spots, Closet items, comments, messages, and your avatar, and the photo you take at a spot to show you've been there ("You at the Spot").

Contacts: only if you choose to find friends from your contacts and allow access. The phone numbers in your address book are sent to our server to check which belong to Landmarkd users. They're used for that check and are not stored.

Messages: direct and group messages you send to other users.

Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, we store your device's push token so we can send them to you.

Activity: a "last active" timestamp so friends can see when you were recently online.

What's public, and what isn't

Public, visible to anyone using the app: your username, display name, bio, and profile photo, the badges you choose to show, the spots you add, your reviews and their photos, your comments, and your likes.

Shared spots: when someone shares a spot, it gets a public web page on landmarkd.co that anyone with the link can open. It shows the spot's name, photos, description, tags, neighbourhood, and average rating. It never shows reviews, usernames, or who added the spot.

Your choice, set under Settings › Privacy: who can see your followers and following lists (Public, Friends only, or Private). The follower and following counts remain visible.

Private, only you can see these: your phone number, your Closet, your saved folders, your chats with Yves, and your "You at the Spot" photos. Your messages are visible only to you and the people in that conversation.

AI features

Yves, outfit suggestions, automatic photo tagging, and moderation (checking comments, and checking spot and review photos so private homes and unrelated photos stay off the map) are powered by Anthropic's Claude API.

When you use these features, the relevant text and images are sent to Anthropic to generate a response. This happens through our own server, so your data is never sent from your device directly to a third party. See Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy for how they handle API data.

Services we rely on

Supabase: hosts our database, accounts, and photo storage.
Anthropic: powers the AI features described above.
Mapbox: address search and walking directions.
Apple Maps: map display on iPhone.
Google Maps: map display on Android devices.
Expo: delivers push notifications (through Apple's and Google's notification services) and app updates.
Open-Meteo: weather, used to tailor outfit suggestions. No personal data is sent.
Sentry: crash reporting, so we find out when the app breaks.

About crash reporting: when the app hits an error, Sentry receives the technical details (what went wrong, which screen, your device model and app version) along with your account's internal ID so we can tell whether a bug is hitting one person repeatedly or many people once. It does not receive your name, email, IP address, or any of your content: no messages, no Closet items, no Yves conversations. We do not record your screen.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Keeping it secure

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is enforced at the database level with per-user security rules, so private content such as your Closet and messages cannot be read by other users even if someone attempts to query our API directly.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your data and your choices

You can edit your username, email, avatar, phone number, and privacy settings at any time under Settings. You can delete individual spots, reviews, comments, and Closet items.

You can delete your account at any time under Settings › Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, reviews, comments, likes, messages, Closet, folders, chats with Yves, and the photos you uploaded. Spots you added stay on the map without your name, because other people's reviews are attached to them.

To request a copy of your data, email support@landmarkd.co and we'll action it.

Note that content you've shared publicly (such as a review on a spot) may remain visible to others until it's removed.

Age requirement

Landmarkd is not intended for anyone under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact support@landmarkd.co and we'll delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "last updated" date above will always reflect the current version, and significant changes will be announced in the app.

Contact
