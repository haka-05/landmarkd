How it works Get the app

Delete your Landmarkd account

Last updated October 13, 2026

In the app

Open Landmarkd, go to Profile › Settings › Delete Account, and confirm twice. Your account is deleted right away.

If you can't get into the app

Email support@landmarkd.co from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What's deleted

Your profile (username, display name, bio, avatar, email and phone number), your reviews and comments, likes, follows, Closet, outfit memory, saved folders, chats with Yves, "You at the Spot" photos, notifications, push tokens, reports you filed, and the photos you uploaded.

Messages: your direct-message conversations are deleted for both people in them, the messages you sent in group chats are deleted, and group chats you created are closed.

What's kept

Spots you added stay on the map, with their photos, without your name, because other people's reviews are attached to them. If you'd like a spot you added removed too, say so in your email.

If someone reported a comment or message of yours, our moderation record keeps the reported text, without your name.
